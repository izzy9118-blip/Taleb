# Founding Charter

## 1. Scope

This charter establishes the Taleb minister house and governs its founding inquiry. The founding act is settled. It authorizes documentary reconstruction only and does not grant operational minister status, grant a voice, certify semantic completion, or register Taleb in Sanctum.

## 2. Corpus before architecture

The inquiry begins with sources, not a model of Taleb assembled from reputation, slogans, famous concepts, or another minister's structure. Candidate architectures may be proposed only as hypotheses.

The native unit of Taleb's reasoning must be discovered from repeated corpus evidence. A candidate unit must recur across distinct primary works or documentary settings, survive counterexamples, and explain more of the evidence than plausible alternatives. Until that showing is made and reviewed, the native unit and final architecture remain `PENDING_PROBE`.

## 3. Source authority

Primary sources govern reconstruction. External material enters only when directly necessary to acquire an exact witness, establish relevant context, or test a specific claim. Unrelated scholarship, criticism, publisher material, promotion, reviews, and commentary are ignored.

Authority is claim-relative. Exact Taleb wording is governed by the exact witness; repeated method requires recurrence across primary works; technical propositions remain answerable to their assumptions and derivations; and market claims remain answerable to timestamped market, instrument, issuer, or regulatory evidence. No source has universal authority outside the claim it can establish.

Every consequential interpretive claim must remain traceable to its evidence and distinguish quotation, documentary fact, inference, hypothesis, and unresolved question.

## 4. Experimental memory

Architectural experiments are expected to fail. Each serious experiment must be preserved as a dated record containing its proposal, evidence, tests, counterevidence, outcome, and successor if any. Correction moves forward through an explicit supersession record. Failed or abandoned structures may not be deleted, silently rewritten, or retroactively made to appear inevitable.

## 5. Prohibited authority claims

No AI system may:

- certify its own reconstruction, completeness, fidelity, readiness, or voice;
- convert structural validation into semantic certification;
- imitate Taleb's voice before a separate voice discipline is evidenced and human-ratified;
- present a founding hypothesis as Taleb's doctrine;
- authorize operational status or Sanctum admission.

Automated checks may validate syntax, paths, schemas, and other mechanically testable conditions. Their passing proves only those conditions.

## 6. Founding and operation

`FOUNDING` and `OPERATIONAL` are distinct states. This repository is `FOUNDING` and `NOT_OPERATIONAL`; its semantic completion is `INCOMPLETE`, and its substantive readiness is `PENDING_PROBE`.

Operational status requires a later, explicit act grounded in an acquired primary corpus, a repeatedly evidenced native reasoning unit, tested method and safeguards, a separately ratified voice discipline if voice is needed, and human review. Sanctum registration is another separate act in the Sanctum repository. Neither transition is implied by the existence or validation of these files.

## 7. Present limits

This charter deliberately leaves the corpus index, method, native unit, architecture, voice discipline, operational contract, and substantive findings unresolved. The founding layer preserves the conditions for discovering them without pretending that discovery has already occurred.
