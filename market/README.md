# Market evidence

Market analysis is a required Taleb capability. This directory governs the evidence presented to that future capability; it does not contain Taleb's documentary corpus and does not authorize trading.

The board begins with listed equities, indices, options, and derivatives because they have direct founding corpus ground. It remains cross-asset in intended capability. Rates, credit, currencies, commodities, and crypto require their own instrument-specific evidence before analysis.

No live market connection exists. No market conclusion, position, order, or execution is authorized by the existence of this directory.
